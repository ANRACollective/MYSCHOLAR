How to Become a Forensic Scientist in Malaysia

Cara Jadi Saintis Forensik di Malaysia

Forensic science attracts more Malaysian students than almost any other science career relative to its actual job openings, so this page owes you the honest version first: the field is real, fascinating and important, and it is also small, government-centred and competitive. The main employer is Jabatan Kimia Malaysia (the Department of Chemistry), whose laboratories analyse drugs, toxicology, DNA and trace evidence for the courts; alongside it sit PDRM's forensic units for crime scene work, hospital forensic medicine departments handling post-mortems under KKM, and niche roles in customs, fire investigation and private laboratories. Degree routes: dedicated forensic science programmes exist at USM's health campus and UiTM among others, but here is the useful secret: many working forensic analysts entered with straight chemistry, biochemistry or biomedical science degrees, because government lab posts recruit on the underlying science, and those degrees keep every other lab career open if forensic openings are scarce the year you graduate. Government scientific officer posts start around RM2,500 to RM3,500 on standard schemes, with the stability, pension and allowances of public service. The realistic strategy we suggest: take the strongest chemistry-heavy degree you can, add forensic electives or a forensic masters later, watch SPA announcements for Jabatan Kimia and KKM intakes, and treat television forensics as inspiration rather than a job description, because the real work is meticulous laboratory science with court testimony, not chases.

Sains forensik menarik lebih ramai pelajar Malaysia berbanding hampir mana-mana kerjaya sains lain jika diukur dengan kekosongan kerja sebenarnya, jadi halaman ini berhutang versi jujur dulu: bidang ini benar, menarik dan penting, dan ia juga kecil, berpusat pada kerajaan dan kompetitif. Majikan utama ialah Jabatan Kimia Malaysia, yang makmalnya menganalisis dadah, toksikologi, DNA dan bukti kesan untuk mahkamah; di sampingnya ada unit forensik PDRM untuk kerja tempat kejadian, jabatan perubatan forensik hospital mengendalikan bedah siasat bawah KKM, dan peranan niche dalam kastam, siasatan kebakaran dan makmal swasta. Laluan ijazah: program sains forensik khusus ada di kampus kesihatan USM dan UiTM antara lain, tetapi ini rahsia berguna: ramai penganalisis forensik yang bekerja masuk dengan ijazah kimia, biokimia atau sains bioperubatan biasa, kerana jawatan makmal kerajaan mengambil berdasarkan sains asasnya, dan ijazah itu mengekalkan semua kerjaya makmal lain terbuka kalau kekosongan forensik sedikit pada tahun anda tamat. Jawatan pegawai sains kerajaan bermula sekitar RM2,500 hingga RM3,500 pada skim standard, dengan kestabilan, pencen dan elaun perkhidmatan awam. Strategi realistik yang kami cadangkan: ambil ijazah berat kimia paling kukuh yang anda mampu, tambah elektif forensik atau sarjana forensik kemudian, pantau pengumuman SPA untuk pengambilan Jabatan Kimia dan KKM, dan anggap forensik televisyen sebagai inspirasi bukan huraian kerja, kerana kerja sebenarnya sains makmal yang teliti dengan keterangan mahkamah, bukan kejar-mengejar.
