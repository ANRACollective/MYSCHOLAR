UniKL and MIED Scholarships - What They Are and How to Apply

Biasiswa UniKL dan MIED - Apa Itu dan Cara Memohon

Students kept searching MyScholar for Yayasan UniKL and MIED and finding nothing, so this page fixes that. They are two separate funders often confused with each other. Yayasan UniKL is the foundation attached to Universiti Kuala Lumpur, the MARA-owned technical university with campuses across the country covering engineering technology, aviation, medical technology and business. The foundation channels zakat, corporate and alumni contributions into financial aid for needy and deserving UniKL students, covering study costs and living support, and applications go through UniKL's student affairs and foundation channels once you are enrolled. Because UniKL is a MARA institution, eligible bumiputera students also access MARA education financing for its programmes, which for many students is the primary funding layer. MIED (Maju Institute of Educational Development) is different: it is the educational arm of MIC, established to expand higher education access for Malaysian Indian students, it founded AIMST University in Kedah, and it provides education loans and scholarships targeted primarily at Indian community students across recognised institutions, with need and merit both weighed. If you are Indian Malaysian and funding is the barrier, MIED is one of the specific doors built for you, and it is underused simply because few students know it exists.

Pelajar asyik mencari Yayasan UniKL dan MIED di MyScholar dan tidak jumpa apa-apa, jadi halaman ini membetulkannya. Ini dua penaja berasingan yang selalu dikelirukan. Yayasan UniKL ialah yayasan di bawah Universiti Kuala Lumpur, universiti teknikal milik MARA dengan kampus di seluruh negara meliputi teknologi kejuruteraan, penerbangan, teknologi perubatan dan perniagaan. Yayasan ini menyalurkan sumbangan zakat, korporat dan alumni kepada bantuan kewangan untuk pelajar UniKL yang memerlukan dan layak, menampung kos pengajian dan sara hidup, dan permohonan melalui saluran hal ehwal pelajar dan yayasan UniKL selepas anda mendaftar. Oleh sebab UniKL institusi MARA, pelajar bumiputera yang layak juga boleh akses pembiayaan pendidikan MARA untuk programnya, yang bagi ramai pelajar menjadi lapisan pembiayaan utama. MIED (Maju Institute of Educational Development) pula berbeza: ia badan pendidikan MIC, ditubuhkan untuk meluaskan akses pendidikan tinggi pelajar India Malaysia, ia mengasaskan AIMST University di Kedah, dan ia menyediakan pinjaman pendidikan dan biasiswa disasarkan terutamanya kepada pelajar komuniti India di institusi yang diiktiraf, dengan keperluan dan merit sama-sama ditimbang. Kalau anda India Malaysia dan pembiayaan jadi penghalang, MIED antara pintu khusus yang dibina untuk anda, dan ia kurang digunakan hanya kerana sedikit pelajar tahu ia wujud.
