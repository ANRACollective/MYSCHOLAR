TESP - The TNB Education Sponsorship Programme, Explained

Biasiswa TESP TNB - Panduan Penuh

TESP has quietly become one of the most typed-in searches on MyScholar, and for good reason: Tenaga Nasional's education sponsorship is among the most complete packages a Malaysian student can get. The programme, run through TNB and Yayasan Tenaga Nasional, sponsors high-performing students through preparatory studies and degrees in fields the utility actually needs, with electrical and power engineering at the core, alongside mechanical, civil and increasingly digital and data fields as the grid modernises. Sponsorship typically covers full tuition, living allowances and related costs, and comes with the trade every student should weigh deliberately: a service bond with TNB after graduation. For many scholars that bond is the best part, a guaranteed engineering job at one of Malaysia's largest companies in an era when the energy transition, grid upgrades and data centre demand are pouring work into the power sector. Selection is built on SPM results (with strong mathematics and science expected), followed by assessments and interviews, and application windows typically open after SPM results season, announced through TNB's official channels. If you are aiming for TESP, the practical preparation is straightforward: protect your SPM maths and physics grades, be ready to explain a genuine interest in the energy sector, and apply the moment the window opens rather than the week it closes. TESP diam-diam menjadi antara carian paling banyak ditaip di MyScholar, dan memang ada sebabnya: tajaan pendidikan Tenaga Nasional antara pakej paling lengkap yang boleh didapati pelajar Malaysia. Program ini, dikendalikan melalui TNB dan Yayasan Tenaga Nasional, menaja pelajar cemerlang melalui pengajian persediaan dan ijazah dalam bidang yang syarikat utiliti itu betul-betul perlukan, dengan kejuruteraan elektrik dan kuasa sebagai teras, di samping mekanikal, awam dan semakin banyak bidang digital dan data seiring pemodenan grid. Tajaan biasanya menanggung yuran penuh, elaun sara hidup dan kos berkaitan, dan datang dengan tukaran yang setiap pelajar patut timbang dengan teliti: ikatan perkhidmatan dengan TNB selepas tamat. Bagi ramai penerima, ikatan itu bahagian terbaik, kerja kejuruteraan terjamin di salah satu syarikat terbesar Malaysia ketika peralihan tenaga, naik taraf grid dan permintaan pusat data mencurahkan kerja ke sektor kuasa. Pemilihan berasaskan keputusan SPM (matematik dan sains yang kukuh dijangka), diikuti penilaian dan temuduga, dan tempoh permohonan biasanya dibuka selepas musim keputusan SPM, diumumkan melalui saluran rasmi TNB. Kalau anda sasarkan TESP, persediaan praktikalnya jelas: jaga gred matematik dan fizik SPM, bersedia menerangkan minat sebenar terhadap sektor tenaga, dan mohon sebaik sahaja tetingkap dibuka, bukan minggu ia tutup. Semak senarai di bawah untuk status kitaran semasa dan pautan permohonan rasmi.
