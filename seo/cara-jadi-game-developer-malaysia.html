How to Become a Game Developer in Malaysia

Cara Jadi Game Developer di Malaysia

Malaysia has a real games industry, not just a dream of one. Studios like Lemon Sky, Passion Republic and Streamline have worked on major international titles, Metronomik proved a Malaysian original can ship worldwide, and MDEC's LEVEL UP KL conference has made KL a recognised meeting point for the Southeast Asian games business. Jobs split into three broad tracks: programming (Unity, Unreal, C++ and C#), art (3D modelling, animation, concept art), and design or production. The programming track usually starts from a computer science or software engineering degree, while dedicated game development programmes exist at APU, KDU (UOW Malaysia), MMU and The One Academy. But this is a portfolio industry before it is a certificate industry: a playable demo, a polished art reel or shipped mods count more than grades in interviews. Starting pay is around RM2,800 a month at local studios, lower than generic software jobs, which is the honest trade-off for working on games. Experienced developers and artists at outsourcing studios serving AAA clients earn considerably more, and remote work for overseas studios has become a real option for senior people. If you want this path, start building something small and finish it. A finished bad game teaches more than an unfinished ambitious one.

Malaysia ada industri game yang sebenar, bukan sekadar angan-angan. Studio seperti Lemon Sky, Passion Republic dan Streamline pernah bekerja pada judul antarabangsa terkenal, Metronomik membuktikan game asli Malaysia boleh dijual ke seluruh dunia, dan persidangan LEVEL UP KL anjuran MDEC menjadikan KL tempat pertemuan industri game Asia Tenggara. Kerja terbahagi kepada tiga laluan besar: pengaturcaraan (Unity, Unreal, C++ dan C#), seni (model 3D, animasi, concept art), dan reka bentuk atau produksi. Laluan pengaturcaraan biasanya bermula dari ijazah sains komputer atau kejuruteraan perisian, manakala program khusus game development ada di APU, KDU (UOW Malaysia), MMU dan The One Academy. Tetapi industri ini pentingkan portfolio lebih daripada sijil: demo yang boleh dimain, art reel yang kemas atau mod yang siap lebih bernilai daripada gred dalam temuduga. Gaji permulaan sekitar RM2,800 sebulan di studio tempatan, lebih rendah daripada kerja perisian biasa, itulah tukaran jujur untuk bekerja dalam bidang game. Pembangun dan artis berpengalaman di studio outsourcing yang melayan klien AAA dapat jauh lebih tinggi, dan kerja remote untuk studio luar negara kini pilihan sebenar bagi yang senior. Kalau nak laluan ini, mula bina sesuatu yang kecil dan siapkan. Game kecil yang siap mengajar lebih banyak daripada game besar yang tergantung.
