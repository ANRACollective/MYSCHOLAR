How to Become a Data Engineer in Malaysia

Cara Jadi Data Engineer di Malaysia

Everyone talks about data scientists, but the quiet truth of the Malaysian job market is that data engineers are scarcer, hired faster and often paid better. The difference matters: a data scientist builds models, while a data engineer builds the pipelines that collect, clean and deliver the data those models need. No pipeline, no model, which is why every bank, telco, airline and e-commerce platform in Malaysia is hiring for this role, and why the AI and data centre wave keeps raising demand. The route in is a computer science, software engineering or data science degree, though strong graduates from maths and other engineering fields make the jump too. The working skill set is concrete: SQL at a genuinely deep level (this is the single most tested skill in interviews), Python, a pipeline orchestrator like Airflow, distributed processing with Spark, and cloud data platforms, with Databricks and the cloud-native warehouses appearing in more Malaysian job ads each year. A practical way to prove yourself without experience: build one end-to-end project that pulls real Malaysian public data, cleans it, loads it into a warehouse and updates on a schedule, then put it on GitHub. Starting pay runs RM4,500 and up, seniors clear RM8,000 to RM12,000, and because the tooling is identical worldwide, remote regional offers come early in this career.

Semua orang cakap tentang data scientist, tetapi kebenaran senyap pasaran kerja Malaysia ialah data engineer lebih jarang, lebih cepat diambil dan selalunya dibayar lebih baik. Bezanya penting: data scientist bina model, manakala data engineer bina pipeline yang mengumpul, membersih dan menghantar data yang model itu perlukan. Tiada pipeline, tiada model, sebab itu setiap bank, telco, syarikat penerbangan dan platform e-dagang di Malaysia sedang mengambil peranan ini, dan gelombang AI serta pusat data terus menaikkan permintaan. Laluan masuk ialah ijazah sains komputer, kejuruteraan perisian atau sains data, walaupun graduan matematik dan kejuruteraan lain yang kukuh turut berjaya beralih. Set kemahiran kerjanya konkrit: SQL pada tahap betul-betul mendalam (ini kemahiran paling banyak diuji dalam temuduga), Python, orchestrator pipeline seperti Airflow, pemprosesan teragih dengan Spark, dan platform data cloud, dengan Databricks dan warehouse cloud-native muncul dalam lebih banyak iklan kerja Malaysia setiap tahun. Cara praktikal buktikan diri tanpa pengalaman: bina satu projek hujung-ke-hujung yang tarik data awam Malaysia sebenar, bersihkan, muatkan ke warehouse dan kemas kini ikut jadual, kemudian letak di GitHub. Gaji permulaan RM4,500 ke atas, senior lepasi RM8,000 hingga RM12,000, dan kerana alatnya sama di seluruh dunia, tawaran remote serantau datang awal dalam kerjaya ini.
