How to Become a Midwife in Malaysia

Cara Jadi Bidan di Malaysia

Midwives care for women through pregnancy, labour, birth and the weeks after, and in Malaysia the profession is regulated by the Malaysian Midwifery Board under the Ministry of Health, with its own register separate from nursing. That last point shapes the whole route. Unlike some countries, Malaysia does not run a direct entry midwifery degree for school leavers in any volume; the recognised path is to qualify and register as a nurse first, through a diploma or degree in nursing at a Ministry of Health college, a public university or an accredited private college like IMU, MAHSA or KPJ, then complete a post basic midwifery programme, typically a six to twelve month advanced diploma, and register with the Midwifery Board. The old community midwife route (Jururawat Masyarakat) still exists for rural health clinics and is a shorter MOH-run programme, which is worth knowing if you want to work in Sabah, Sarawak or the rural peninsula. Entry at SPM level means credits in Biology, Chemistry and a third science alongside BM and English, and MOH nursing colleges carry an allowance during training plus a bond. Pay as a registered midwife starts at RM3,000 to RM4,600 a month in government and private maternity wards, with the government scale adding allowances for shifts and critical care. Demand is steady rather than booming: MOH maternity units keep consistent staffing, private hospitals in the Klang Valley, Penang and Johor recruit regularly, and Malaysia's maternal health targets under the 12th Malaysia Plan keep the role funded. The honest part is the hours. Babies arrive at 3am, and a midwife's week does not look like an office week.

Bidan jaga wanita sepanjang kehamilan, bersalin, kelahiran dan minggu-minggu selepasnya, dan di Malaysia profesion ini dikawal selia oleh Lembaga Bidan Malaysia di bawah Kementerian Kesihatan, dengan daftar sendiri yang berasingan daripada kejururawatan. Perkara terakhir itu membentuk keseluruhan laluan. Tidak seperti sesetengah negara, Malaysia tidak jalankan ijazah kebidanan kemasukan terus untuk lepasan sekolah dalam jumlah besar; laluan yang diiktiraf ialah layak dan daftar sebagai jururawat dulu, melalui diploma atau ijazah kejururawatan di kolej Kementerian Kesihatan, universiti awam atau kolej swasta yang diakreditasi seperti IMU, MAHSA atau KPJ, kemudian tamatkan program post basic kebidanan, biasanya diploma lanjutan enam hingga dua belas bulan, dan daftar dengan Lembaga Bidan. Laluan lama Jururawat Masyarakat masih wujud untuk klinik kesihatan luar bandar dan ia program KKM yang lebih pendek, yang patut tahu kalau anda mahu bekerja di Sabah, Sarawak atau luar bandar semenanjung. Kemasukan peringkat SPM bermakna kredit Biologi, Kimia dan satu lagi sains bersama BM dan Bahasa Inggeris, dan kolej kejururawatan KKM ada elaun semasa latihan serta ikatan. Gaji sebagai bidan berdaftar bermula RM3,000 hingga RM4,600 sebulan di wad bersalin kerajaan dan swasta, dengan skala kerajaan tambah elaun syif dan rawatan kritikal. Permintaan stabil, bukan meletup: unit bersalin KKM kekalkan kakitangan yang konsisten, hospital swasta di Lembah Klang, Pulau Pinang dan Johor ambil pekerja secara berkala, dan sasaran kesihatan ibu Malaysia di bawah Rancangan Malaysia Ke-12 kekalkan pembiayaan peranan ini. Bahagian jujurnya ialah waktu kerja. Bayi lahir pukul 3 pagi, dan minggu bidan tak sama seperti minggu pejabat.
