How to Become an AI Engineer in Malaysia

Cara Jadi Jurutera AI di Malaysia

AI engineering has moved from a niche role to one of the most recruited tech jobs in Malaysia. The national AI agenda, the wave of data centres being built in Johor and Cyberjaya, and banks and GLCs building their own AI teams have all pushed demand well past supply. The usual pathway is a degree in computer science, data science, or AI itself. UM, USM, UTM and UKM all offer AI or data science programmes, and private universities like APU, MMU, Sunway and Taylor's have dedicated AI degrees. What employers actually test for is practical skill: Python, machine learning frameworks like PyTorch or TensorFlow, and evidence you have built something real, whether a final year project, a Kaggle profile, or a working model deployed somewhere. Starting pay for fresh graduates is around RM4,500 a month, and engineers with two to three years of experience in Kuala Lumpur or Penang regularly cross RM8,000. A formal AI degree is the smoothest route, but a strong maths or engineering graduate who self-learns the ML stack and can show projects is hired all the time. Scholarships for computer science and data science count toward this path, and several GLC scholarships now name AI and data as priority fields.

Kejuruteraan AI sudah menjadi antara kerja teknologi paling banyak diambil di Malaysia. Agenda AI negara, pembinaan pusat data secara besar-besaran di Johor dan Cyberjaya, serta bank dan GLC yang membina pasukan AI sendiri menyebabkan permintaan jauh melebihi bekalan. Laluan biasa ialah ijazah sains komputer, sains data, atau AI sendiri. UM, USM, UTM dan UKM semua ada program AI atau sains data, dan universiti swasta seperti APU, MMU, Sunway dan Taylor's ada ijazah AI khusus. Apa yang majikan uji sebenarnya ialah skil praktikal: Python, rangka kerja machine learning seperti PyTorch atau TensorFlow, dan bukti anda pernah bina sesuatu yang berfungsi, sama ada projek tahun akhir, profil Kaggle, atau model yang sudah digunakan. Gaji permulaan graduan baru sekitar RM4,500 sebulan, dan jurutera dengan dua hingga tiga tahun pengalaman di KL atau Pulau Pinang selalu melepasi RM8,000. Ijazah AI formal ialah laluan paling lancar, tetapi graduan matematik atau kejuruteraan yang belajar sendiri stack ML dan boleh tunjuk projek turut diambil bekerja. Biasiswa sains komputer dan sains data sah untuk laluan ini, dan beberapa biasiswa GLC kini menyenaraikan AI dan data sebagai bidang keutamaan.
