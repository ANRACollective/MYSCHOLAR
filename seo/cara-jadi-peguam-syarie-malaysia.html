How to Become a Syariah Lawyer in Malaysia

Cara Jadi Peguam Syarie di Malaysia

Malaysia runs two court systems side by side, and the syariah courts, which handle Islamic family law, inheritance, and religious matters for Muslims, need their own advocates: peguam syarie. The pathway differs from civil law in ways every prospective student should understand early. The academic base is a syariah or Islamic law qualification, with the recognised programmes at IIUM (UIA), USIM, UiTM, UM and UKM, and some students take double degrees combining civil law and syariah, the most flexible qualification in the market because it opens both court systems. Admission then happens state by state: each state's Majlis Agama Islam sets its own requirements for admission as a peguam syarie, typically involving a syariah legal qualification, an exam or interview, and for some states practical experience, so a lawyer admitted in Selangor must apply separately to practise in Johor. The daily work is family law at its most human: divorce (cerai), custody (hadhanah), maintenance (nafkah), inheritance distribution (faraid) and wills (wasiat), plus a growing commercial side as Islamic finance disputes and muamalat matters expand. Fresh syariah lawyers in small firms start around RM3,000, established practitioners in the Klang Valley earn RM5,000 and well beyond, and the double-qualified civil-and-syarie lawyers command the strongest positions. One practical note: strong Bahasa and Arabic legal literacy matter here more than in civil practice, and courtroom advocacy in BM is the daily craft.

Malaysia menjalankan dua sistem mahkamah seiring, dan mahkamah syariah, yang mengendalikan undang-undang kekeluargaan Islam, pewarisan dan hal ehwal agama bagi orang Islam, memerlukan peguamnya sendiri: peguam syarie. Laluannya berbeza daripada undang-undang sivil dalam cara yang setiap bakal pelajar patut faham awal. Asas akademiknya kelayakan syariah atau undang-undang Islam, dengan program yang diiktiraf di UIAM (UIA), USIM, UiTM, UM dan UKM, dan sesetengah pelajar ambil ijazah berkembar menggabungkan undang-undang sivil dan syariah, kelayakan paling fleksibel dalam pasaran kerana ia membuka kedua-dua sistem mahkamah. Kemasukan kemudian berlaku negeri demi negeri: Majlis Agama Islam setiap negeri menetapkan syarat sendiri untuk diterima sebagai peguam syarie, biasanya melibatkan kelayakan undang-undang syariah, peperiksaan atau temuduga, dan bagi sesetengah negeri pengalaman praktikal, jadi peguam yang diterima di Selangor mesti memohon berasingan untuk beramal di Johor. Kerja hariannya undang-undang keluarga pada sisi paling manusiawi: cerai, hadhanah, nafkah, faraid dan wasiat, serta sisi komersial yang berkembang apabila pertikaian kewangan Islam dan hal muamalat meluas. Peguam syarie baru di firma kecil mula sekitar RM3,000, pengamal mapan di Lembah Klang dapat RM5,000 dan jauh lebih, dan peguam berkelayakan dua sivil-dan-syarie pegang kedudukan paling kukuh. Satu nota praktikal: penguasaan Bahasa dan literasi undang-undang Arab lebih penting di sini berbanding amalan sivil, dan hujahan mahkamah dalam BM ialah kraf harian.
