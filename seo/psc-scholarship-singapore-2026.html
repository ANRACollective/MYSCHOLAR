PSC Scholarship Singapore 2026 - The Complete Guide

Biasiswa PSC Singapura 2026 - Panduan Lengkap

The Public Service Commission scholarship is the most prestigious undergraduate award in Singapore, and for many of the country's top A Level and IB graduates it is the goal they have worked toward for years. It funds full study costs at leading universities in Singapore or overseas, including Oxbridge and the Ivy League, plus allowances, and in return scholars serve a bond in the Singapore public service, typically four to six years depending on where and what they study. PSC scholars are groomed for leadership across ministries, and many of Singapore's permanent secretaries and political leaders started here. Selection is famously demanding: outstanding academic results are only the entry ticket, and the process weighs co-curricular leadership, psychometric assessments, and a panel interview with the commissioners that probes character, motivation and views on public issues. Applications open after A Level results are released, with the cycle running early in the year. Alongside the open PSC scholarship sit specialised tracks covering the SAF, Home Team, foreign service and engineering. If you are a Singaporean student weighing this path, the honest questions are about the bond: six years in public service shapes your twenties. Scholars who thrive are the ones who actually want that work, not just the brand.

Biasiswa Public Service Commission ialah anugerah prasiswazah paling berprestij di Singapura, dan bagi ramai graduan A Level dan IB terbaik negara itu, ia matlamat yang diusahakan bertahun-tahun. Ia membiayai kos penuh pengajian di universiti terkemuka di Singapura atau luar negara, termasuk Oxbridge dan Ivy League, berserta elaun, dan sebagai balasan penerima berkhidmat dengan ikatan dalam perkhidmatan awam Singapura, biasanya empat hingga enam tahun bergantung pada tempat dan bidang pengajian. Penerima PSC dibentuk untuk kepimpinan merentas kementerian, dan ramai setiausaha tetap serta pemimpin politik Singapura bermula di sini. Pemilihan terkenal ketat: keputusan akademik cemerlang hanya tiket masuk, dan prosesnya menilai kepimpinan kokurikulum, penilaian psikometrik, dan temuduga panel bersama pesuruhjaya yang menguji sahsiah, motivasi dan pandangan tentang isu awam. Permohonan dibuka selepas keputusan A Level diumumkan, dengan kitaran berjalan awal tahun. Di samping biasiswa PSC terbuka ada trek khusus meliputi SAF, Home Team, perkhidmatan luar negeri dan kejuruteraan. Kalau anda pelajar Singapura menimbang laluan ini, soalan jujurnya tentang ikatan: enam tahun dalam perkhidmatan awam membentuk usia dua puluhan anda. Penerima yang berjaya ialah mereka yang betul-betul mahukan kerja itu, bukan sekadar jenamanya.
