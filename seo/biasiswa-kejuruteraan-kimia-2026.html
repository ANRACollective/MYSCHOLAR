Chemical Engineering Scholarships in Malaysia

Biasiswa Kejuruteraan Kimia di Malaysia

Chemical engineering is one of the better funded degrees in Malaysia and one of the worst labelled. Students search for it by name and find one or two records, when in reality most of the country's largest sponsors fund it every year under a different heading. Petronas is the obvious one: its education sponsorship programme has long treated chemical and process engineering as core fields, both at Universiti Teknologi Petronas and at partner universities abroad, and it remains the single largest employer of chemical engineers in the country. Shell, the Sarawak energy and gas players, and the downstream petrochemical groups around Kerteh, Gebeng and Pengerang follow the same pattern, listing their scholarships as engineering or oil and gas rather than chemical. The public universities with strong programmes, including UTM, USM, UMPSA in Pahang and UKM, run merit tuition waivers that cover chemical engineering without naming it. So the practical advice is to search wider than the degree name: engineering, oil and gas, Petronas, and the general GLC scholarships are where the money sits. The field itself holds up well for Malaysia: the petrochemical complexes, palm oil processing, pharmaceuticals in Penang and Selangor, and the newer battery and semiconductor materials plants all hire process engineers, and the Board of Engineers Malaysia professional route is clear. The listings below start from the records that mention chemical engineering directly; use the related searches to widen the net.

Kejuruteraan kimia antara ijazah yang paling banyak dibiayai di Malaysia dan antara yang paling teruk dilabel. Pelajar mencarinya mengikut nama dan jumpa satu dua rekod, sedangkan sebenarnya kebanyakan penaja terbesar negara membiayainya setiap tahun di bawah tajuk lain. Petronas yang paling jelas: program tajaan pendidikannya sudah lama menganggap kejuruteraan kimia dan proses sebagai bidang teras, di Universiti Teknologi Petronas dan di universiti rakan luar negara, dan ia kekal majikan tunggal terbesar jurutera kimia di negara ini. Shell, pemain tenaga dan gas Sarawak, serta kumpulan petrokimia hiliran sekitar Kerteh, Gebeng dan Pengerang ikut corak yang sama, menyenaraikan biasiswa mereka sebagai kejuruteraan atau minyak dan gas, bukan kimia. Universiti awam dengan program yang kukuh, termasuk UTM, USM, UMPSA di Pahang dan UKM, ada pengecualian yuran merit yang merangkumi kejuruteraan kimia tanpa menamakannya. Jadi nasihat praktikalnya ialah cari lebih luas daripada nama ijazah: kejuruteraan, minyak dan gas, Petronas, dan biasiswa GLC umum ialah tempat wangnya. Bidang ini sendiri kukuh untuk Malaysia: kompleks petrokimia, pemprosesan minyak sawit, farmaseutikal di Pulau Pinang dan Selangor, serta loji bahan bateri dan semikonduktor yang baru semuanya mengambil jurutera proses, dan laluan profesional Lembaga Jurutera Malaysia jelas. Senarai di bawah bermula daripada rekod yang sebut kejuruteraan kimia secara langsung; guna carian berkaitan untuk luaskan jaring.
