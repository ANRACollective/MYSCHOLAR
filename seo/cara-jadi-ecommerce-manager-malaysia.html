How to Become an E-commerce Manager in Malaysia

Cara Jadi E-commerce Manager di Malaysia

E-commerce management is one of the few business careers in Malaysia where a 24-year-old can genuinely run a revenue line, because the platforms are young, the playbook changes monthly, and experience on Shopee, Lazada and TikTok Shop counts for more than seniority. The job is running a brand's online retail operation end to end: listings and content, pricing and promotions, campaign calendars around the mega sales (11.11, 12.12, Raya and payday cycles), performance ads inside and outside the platforms, inventory coordination with the warehouse, and the customer chat that decides your store rating. Routes in are flexible. A marketing or business degree is the common base, and platform-side roles at Shopee or Lazada as account managers teach the mechanics faster than anywhere, but the most convincing candidates often started by running a store themselves: their own small brand, a family business they digitised, or a campus dropship experiment with real numbers to show. That last point is the practical advice: you can build the portfolio for this career with RM500 of stock while still studying. Starting salaries for e-commerce executives sit around RM2,500 to RM3,500, managers running full store P&L earn RM3,500 to RM5,500, and heads of e-commerce at established brands go well beyond, with TikTok Shop expertise currently the scarcest and most rewarded skill in the market.

Pengurusan e-dagang antara kerjaya perniagaan di Malaysia yang membolehkan orang berumur 24 tahun betul-betul mengurus satu barisan pendapatan, kerana platformnya muda, playbook berubah setiap bulan, dan pengalaman di Shopee, Lazada dan TikTok Shop lebih bernilai daripada senioriti. Kerjanya mengurus operasi runcit online sesuatu jenama dari hujung ke hujung: listing dan kandungan, harga dan promosi, kalendar kempen sekitar mega sale (11.11, 12.12, Raya dan kitaran gaji), iklan prestasi dalam dan luar platform, koordinasi inventori dengan gudang, dan chat pelanggan yang menentukan rating kedai anda. Laluan masuknya fleksibel. Ijazah pemasaran atau perniagaan asas biasa, dan peranan di pihak platform seperti account manager di Shopee atau Lazada mengajar mekaniknya paling cepat, tetapi calon paling meyakinkan selalunya mula dengan mengurus kedai sendiri: jenama kecil sendiri, perniagaan keluarga yang mereka digitalkan, atau eksperimen dropship kampus dengan angka sebenar untuk ditunjuk. Itu nasihat praktikalnya: anda boleh bina portfolio kerjaya ini dengan stok RM500 sambil masih belajar. Gaji permulaan eksekutif e-dagang sekitar RM2,500 hingga RM3,500, manager yang pegang P&L penuh kedai dapat RM3,500 hingga RM5,500, dan ketua e-dagang di jenama mapan jauh melebihi itu, dengan kepakaran TikTok Shop kini kemahiran paling jarang dan paling diberi ganjaran dalam pasaran.
