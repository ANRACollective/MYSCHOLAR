Aircraft Maintenance Engineering: The Scholarship Route Nobody Searches Correctly

Aircraft Maintenance Engineering: Laluan Biasiswa Yang Jarang Dicari Dengan Betul

Every student who types aircraft into MyScholar gets pilot results, and most of them are not looking for a pilot programme. They want the other side of the hangar: the licensed aircraft maintenance engineer (AME) who signs off an aircraft as airworthy, a career that Malaysia is genuinely short of. The country runs one of Southeast Asia's largest maintenance, repair and overhaul (MRO) sectors, anchored by Malaysia Airlines Engineering, AirAsia's engineering arm, Sepang Aircraft Engineering and a cluster of engine and component shops around KLIA and Subang, and all of them need Part-66 licence holders. The route is not a normal engineering degree. You train towards the CAAM Part-66 licence, usually through a diploma or degree programme at an approved Part-147 training organisation, with UniKL MIAT in Sepang the best known, then build the hands-on hours that the licence requires. Funding works differently from the pilot track too. MARA sponsors a meaningful share of UniKL MIAT students, some airlines and MRO companies run their own cadet technician schemes, ADMAL Aviation College offers partial and full scholarships across its programmes, and the TVET route through TVETMARA keeps the cost low for certificate level entry. Starting pay for a licensed AME is modest, in the RM3,500 to RM5,500 range that the MyJourney aerospace engineer page lists, but licensed engineers with type ratings are paid well above that and are in demand across the Gulf and Singapore. Setiap pelajar yang taip aircraft di MyScholar dapat keputusan juruterbang, dan kebanyakannya bukan mencari program juruterbang. Mereka mahu sisi hangar yang satu lagi: jurutera penyelenggaraan pesawat berlesen (AME) yang mengesahkan pesawat layak terbang, kerjaya yang Malaysia betul-betul kekurangan. Negara ini menjalankan salah satu sektor penyelenggaraan, pembaikan dan baik pulih (MRO) terbesar di Asia Tenggara, berteraskan Malaysia Airlines Engineering, bahagian kejuruteraan AirAsia, Sepang Aircraft Engineering dan kelompok bengkel enjin dan komponen sekitar KLIA dan Subang, dan semuanya perlukan pemegang lesen Part-66. Laluannya bukan ijazah kejuruteraan biasa. Anda berlatih ke arah lesen CAAM Part-66, biasanya melalui program diploma atau ijazah di organisasi latihan Part-147 yang diluluskan, dengan UniKL MIAT di Sepang yang paling dikenali, kemudian kumpul jam praktikal yang lesen itu perlukan. Pembiayaan pun berbeza daripada laluan juruterbang. MARA menaja sebahagian besar pelajar UniKL MIAT, beberapa syarikat penerbangan dan MRO ada skim kadet juruteknik sendiri, ADMAL Aviation College tawarkan biasiswa separa dan penuh merentasi programnya, dan laluan TVET melalui TVETMARA kekalkan kos rendah untuk kemasukan peringkat sijil. Gaji permulaan AME berlesen sederhana, sekitar RM3,500 hingga RM5,500 seperti yang disenaraikan halaman jurutera aeroangkasa MyJourney, tetapi jurutera berlesen dengan type rating dibayar jauh lebih tinggi dan diperlukan di Teluk dan Singapura. Senarai di bawah ialah setiap biasiswa penerbangan dan pesawat di MyScholar, juruterbang dan kejuruteraan kedua-duanya, supaya anda nampak yang mana betul-betul milik anda.
