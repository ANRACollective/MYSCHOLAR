How to Become Cabin Crew in Malaysia

Cara Jadi Pramugari atau Pramugara di Malaysia

Cabin crew is one of the few careers in Malaysia where you can start flying internationally with an SPM certificate, and competition for every intake is intense. The core requirements across Malaysia Airlines, AirAsia and Batik Air are similar: minimum age 18, SPM with credits that must include English, confident spoken English, a minimum height requirement (commonly around 157cm for women and 165cm for men, checked with an arm reach test), good eyesight with or without correction, a clean complexion for visible areas in uniform, and the ability to swim. Recruitment usually happens through walk-in interview days announced on airline careers pages and social media. The day typically involves grooming checks, height and reach measurement, group activities and an English conversation stage. Basic salary for new crew is modest, often around RM1,500 to RM2,000, but flight and layover allowances change the picture: active crew commonly take home RM3,500 to RM5,000 a month, more on international routes. Training is paid and takes a few months, covering safety, first aid and service. It is a genuine career track too: senior crew, leading stewardess and inflight supervisor roles carry real pay bumps, and many crew later move into ground operations or training roles.

Pramugari ialah antara kerjaya di Malaysia yang boleh bermula terbang ke luar negara dengan kelayakan SPM sahaja, dan persaingan setiap pengambilan sangat sengit. Syarat asas MAS, AirAsia dan Batik Air lebih kurang sama: umur minimum 18 tahun, SPM dengan kredit termasuk Bahasa Inggeris, boleh bertutur Bahasa Inggeris dengan yakin, syarat ketinggian minimum (biasanya sekitar 157cm untuk wanita dan 165cm untuk lelaki, disemak dengan ujian capaian tangan), penglihatan baik, kulit bersih pada bahagian yang nampak dalam uniform, dan mesti boleh berenang. Pengambilan biasanya melalui sesi temuduga walk-in yang diumumkan di laman kerjaya syarikat penerbangan dan media sosial. Hari temuduga biasanya ada semakan penampilan, ukur ketinggian dan capaian, aktiviti berkumpulan dan sesi perbualan Bahasa Inggeris. Gaji pokok kru baru memang rendah, sekitar RM1,500 hingga RM2,000, tetapi elaun penerbangan dan layover mengubah keadaan: kru aktif biasanya bawa balik RM3,500 hingga RM5,000 sebulan, lebih tinggi untuk laluan antarabangsa. Latihan dibayar dan mengambil masa beberapa bulan, meliputi keselamatan, bantuan kecemasan dan servis. Ia laluan kerjaya sebenar: jawatan kru kanan dan penyelia penerbangan bawa kenaikan gaji, dan ramai kru kemudian beralih ke operasi darat atau latihan.
