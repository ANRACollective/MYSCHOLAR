How to Become a Wealth Manager in Malaysia

Cara Jadi Wealth Manager di Malaysia

Wealth managers look after the money of people who have a lot of it: business owners, professionals and families who need someone to structure their investments across equities, bonds, unit trusts, insurance and estate planning. In Malaysia the demand has grown sharply in the last few years as KL positioned itself as a regional wealth centre and a string of global family offices set up local operations, so the private banks, the wealth arms of Maybank, CIMB, Public Bank and Hong Leong, and the independent advisory firms are all hiring. The route is more regulated than most finance careers. You need a degree, usually finance, economics, accounting or business, and then a licence before you can legally give investment advice: the Securities Industry Development Corporation (SIDC) examinations that lead to a Capital Markets Services Representative's Licence, and for insurance products the relevant Bank Negara registrations. Most people enter through a bank graduate or management associate programme as a relationship manager trainee, which is where the licensing gets done on the bank's time. The Certified Financial Planner (CFP) qualification is the credential that sets established wealth managers apart. Starting pay is RM4,000 to RM6,000 a month as an associate or trainee relationship manager, but the real money in this career is variable: once you carry a book of clients, bonuses tied to assets under management can double the base. The honest caution is that this is a sales job dressed as an advisory one, at least for the first five years, and your income depends on building trust with people who are rightly suspicious of anyone who wants to manage their money.

Wealth manager jaga wang orang yang ada banyak wang: pemilik perniagaan, profesional dan keluarga yang perlukan seseorang untuk susun pelaburan mereka merentasi ekuiti, bon, unit amanah, insurans dan perancangan harta pusaka. Di Malaysia permintaannya naik mendadak sejak beberapa tahun kebelakangan apabila KL meletakkan dirinya sebagai pusat kekayaan serantau dan rentetan pejabat keluarga global buka operasi tempatan, jadi bank swasta, bahagian kekayaan Maybank, CIMB, Public Bank dan Hong Leong, serta firma nasihat bebas semuanya mengambil pekerja. Laluannya lebih dikawal selia daripada kebanyakan kerjaya kewangan. Anda perlu ijazah, biasanya kewangan, ekonomi, perakaunan atau perniagaan, kemudian lesen sebelum boleh beri nasihat pelaburan secara sah: peperiksaan Securities Industry Development Corporation (SIDC) yang membawa kepada Lesen Wakil Perkhidmatan Pasaran Modal, dan untuk produk insurans, pendaftaran Bank Negara yang berkaitan. Kebanyakan orang masuk melalui program graduan atau management associate bank sebagai pelatih relationship manager, dan di situlah pelesenan dibuat atas masa bank. Kelayakan Certified Financial Planner (CFP) ialah sijil yang membezakan wealth manager yang mantap. Gaji permulaan ialah RM4,000 hingga RM6,000 sebulan sebagai associate atau pelatih relationship manager, tetapi wang sebenar dalam kerjaya ini berubah-ubah: sebaik anda ada buku pelanggan sendiri, bonus yang terikat pada aset di bawah pengurusan boleh gandakan gaji asas. Amaran jujurnya ialah ini kerja jualan yang berpakaian nasihat, sekurang-kurangnya lima tahun pertama, dan pendapatan anda bergantung pada membina kepercayaan dengan orang yang wajar curiga terhadap sesiapa yang mahu uruskan wang mereka.
