How to Become a Special Education Teacher in Malaysia

Cara Jadi Guru Pendidikan Khas di Malaysia

Special education is the teaching track where Malaysia's need most visibly outruns supply. Every year more students are diagnosed and registered with learning differences (autism, ADHD, dyslexia, hearing and visual impairment), the ministry keeps expanding PPKI integration programmes in mainstream schools and inclusive education targets, and schools across the country report shortages of trained pendidikan khas teachers. The routes in mirror general teaching but with a specialised stream. School leavers with strong SPM results can apply to the PISMP programme at IPG teacher institutes with a pendidikan khas option, studying tuition-free with a monthly allowance and a guaranteed posting into government service. At university level, UPSI is the historic centre for special education degrees, with UKM, UM and IIUM offering programmes and postgraduate diplomas that let existing teachers convert into the field, and the ISMP sponsorship covers education degrees for bonded service. The material difference from regular teaching: special education teachers in government schools receive the Bayaran Insentif Pendidikan Khas, a monthly special-education incentive on top of the standard DG-scheme teacher salary, in recognition of the specialised work. Classes are smaller, progress is measured in different units (a nonverbal child's first requested sentence can be a term's triumph), and the emotional demands are real, which is why the best candidates combine patience with genuine structure. For students who want teaching with deeper specialisation, better staffing ratios and an incentive allowance, this is the strongest teaching niche in the country.

Pendidikan khas ialah trek perguruan di mana keperluan Malaysia paling ketara mengatasi bekalan. Setiap tahun lebih ramai murid didiagnosis dan didaftar dengan perbezaan pembelajaran (autisme, ADHD, disleksia, masalah pendengaran dan penglihatan), kementerian terus meluaskan program integrasi PPKI di sekolah arus perdana dan sasaran pendidikan inklusif, dan sekolah di seluruh negara melaporkan kekurangan guru pendidikan khas terlatih. Laluan masuknya seperti perguruan biasa tetapi dengan aliran khusus. Lepasan sekolah dengan keputusan SPM kukuh boleh memohon program PISMP di institut perguruan IPG dengan pilihan pendidikan khas, belajar tanpa yuran dengan elaun bulanan dan penempatan terjamin ke perkhidmatan kerajaan. Di peringkat universiti, UPSI ialah pusat bersejarah ijazah pendidikan khas, dengan UKM, UM dan IIUM menawarkan program serta diploma lepasan ijazah yang membolehkan guru sedia ada beralih ke bidang ini, dan tajaan ISMP menampung ijazah pendidikan dengan khidmat berikat. Beza material daripada perguruan biasa: guru pendidikan khas di sekolah kerajaan menerima Bayaran Insentif Pendidikan Khas, insentif bulanan di atas gaji guru skim DG standard, sebagai pengiktirafan kerja khusus itu. Kelas lebih kecil, kemajuan diukur dalam unit berbeza (ayat pertama yang diminta seorang kanak-kanak bukan lisan boleh jadi kejayaan satu penggal), dan tuntutan emosinya nyata, sebab itu calon terbaik menggabungkan kesabaran dengan struktur yang tulen. Untuk pelajar yang mahu perguruan dengan pengkhususan lebih mendalam, nisbah guru-murid lebih baik dan elaun insentif, ini niche perguruan paling kukuh di negara ini.
