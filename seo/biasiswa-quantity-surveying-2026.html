Quantity Surveying Scholarships in Malaysia

Biasiswa Ukur Bahan di Malaysia

Quantity surveying is the construction industry's accounting profession: QS graduates measure, cost and control the money on every building, highway and plant, and in Malaysia the career runs through the Board of Quantity Surveyors Malaysia (BQSM) registration. It is also one of the few degrees where the sponsors are not banks or government but the developers and contractors themselves, who need a steady supply of QS graduates and fund them directly. On MyScholar the four active sponsors that name quantity surveying are all from that world. The Eco World Foundation scholarship pays RM8,000 to RM20,000 a year plus a living allowance with no bond and B40 priority, and its 2026 round closes on 31 October, so if you are reading this in the last week of October, go now. IJM Corporation covers full tuition and allowances with a bond and rolling intakes. WCT Holdings runs an annual bonded scholarship covering tuition. And the SP Setia Foundation offers full tuition, a living allowance and a laptop, bonded, opening around the second quarter. Gamuda, Sunway and the state housing boards sponsor into the field too under broader construction or built environment headings. The degree is offered at UM, UTM, USM, UiTM, UTAR, Taylor's and INTI among others, and most programmes are accredited by BQSM and the RICS, which matters because Malaysian QS graduates work across Singapore, the Gulf and Australia on the strength of that recognition. Starting pay is around RM2,800 to RM3,800 and rises with chartered status. The listings below are the four direct sponsors; the related searches cover construction and built environment more broadly.

Ukur bahan ialah profesion perakaunan industri pembinaan: graduan QS mengukur, menilai kos dan mengawal wang untuk setiap bangunan, lebuh raya dan loji, dan di Malaysia kerjaya ini melalui pendaftaran Lembaga Juruukur Bahan Malaysia (BQSM). Ia juga antara sedikit ijazah yang penajanya bukan bank atau kerajaan tetapi pemaju dan kontraktor sendiri, yang perlukan bekalan graduan QS yang stabil dan biayai mereka secara langsung. Di MyScholar, empat penaja aktif yang menamakan ukur bahan semuanya dari dunia itu. Biasiswa Eco World Foundation bayar RM8,000 hingga RM20,000 setahun serta elaun sara hidup tanpa ikatan dan keutamaan B40, dan pusingan 2026 tutup pada 31 Oktober, jadi kalau anda baca ini pada minggu terakhir Oktober, pergi sekarang. IJM Corporation tanggung yuran penuh dan elaun dengan ikatan dan kemasukan bergolek. WCT Holdings jalankan biasiswa tahunan berikat yang tanggung yuran. Dan Yayasan SP Setia tawarkan yuran penuh, elaun sara hidup dan komputer riba, berikat, dibuka sekitar suku kedua. Gamuda, Sunway dan lembaga perumahan negeri juga menaja bidang ini di bawah tajuk pembinaan atau alam bina yang lebih luas. Ijazah ini ditawarkan di UM, UTM, USM, UiTM, UTAR, Taylor's dan INTI antara lain, dan kebanyakan program diakreditasi BQSM dan RICS, yang penting sebab graduan QS Malaysia bekerja di Singapura, Teluk dan Australia atas pengiktirafan itu. Gaji permulaan sekitar RM2,800 hingga RM3,800 dan naik dengan status chartered. Senarai di bawah ialah empat penaja langsung; carian berkaitan merangkumi pembinaan dan alam bina secara lebih luas.
