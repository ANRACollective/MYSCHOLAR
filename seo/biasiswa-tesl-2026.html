TESL Scholarships in Malaysia - Every Funded Route to Teaching English

Biasiswa TESL di Malaysia - Semua Laluan Bertaja untuk Mengajar Bahasa Inggeris

TESL (Teaching English as a Second Language) is one of the most repeatedly searched scholarship terms on MyScholar, and the answer deserves a proper page. The main sponsored route is the Ministry of Education's ISMP (Ijazah Sarjana Muda Pendidikan) sponsorship, which funds teaching degrees including TESL at partner universities, pays study allowances, and bonds graduates into government school service, effectively guaranteeing a teaching post. Alongside it, the IPG (Institut Pendidikan Guru) network trains teachers through the PISMP route, where selected SPM leavers study tuition-free with a monthly allowance; TESL is consistently one of the most competitive options in each intake, and strong SPM English plus a convincing interview matter most. At university level, TESL and English language education degrees run at UKM, UPM, UiTM, IIUM, UPSI and many private universities, fundable through JPA, state foundations and PTPTN. Beyond teaching in schools, a TESL degree opens doors in private language centres, corporate training, publishing and edtech, so a bond-free route keeps more options open while a bonded route buys certainty. If English is your strongest subject and you want it to be your career, there are more funded ways in than almost any other arts-stream path in Malaysia.

TESL antara istilah biasiswa paling kerap dicari di MyScholar, dan jawapannya layak diberi halaman sendiri. Laluan tajaan utama ialah tajaan ISMP (Ijazah Sarjana Muda Pendidikan) Kementerian Pendidikan, yang membiayai ijazah perguruan termasuk TESL di universiti rakan, membayar elaun pengajian, dan mengikat graduan ke perkhidmatan sekolah kerajaan, sekali gus hampir menjamin jawatan guru. Selain itu, rangkaian IPG (Institut Pendidikan Guru) melatih guru melalui laluan PISMP, di mana lepasan SPM terpilih belajar tanpa yuran dengan elaun bulanan; TESL sentiasa antara pilihan paling kompetitif setiap pengambilan, dan Bahasa Inggeris SPM yang cemerlang serta temuduga yang meyakinkan paling penting. Di peringkat universiti, ijazah TESL dan pendidikan bahasa Inggeris ada di UKM, UPM, UiTM, IIUM, UPSI dan banyak universiti swasta, boleh dibiayai melalui JPA, yayasan negeri dan PTPTN. Selain mengajar di sekolah, ijazah TESL membuka pintu ke pusat bahasa swasta, latihan korporat, penerbitan dan edtech, jadi laluan tanpa ikatan mengekalkan lebih banyak pilihan manakala laluan berikat membeli kepastian. Kalau Bahasa Inggeris subjek terkuat anda dan anda mahu jadikannya kerjaya, ada lebih banyak laluan bertaja ke bidang ini berbanding hampir mana-mana laluan aliran sastera lain di Malaysia.
