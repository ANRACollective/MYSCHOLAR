Tourism and Hospitality Scholarships in Malaysia

Biasiswa Pelancongan dan Hospitaliti di Malaysia

Tourism searches kept returning zero results on MyScholar, which told us students want this page badly, so here is the honest map. Malaysia's tourism industry is hiring again in force, with Visit Malaysia campaigns, new hotel openings and airline expansion all pulling in fresh graduates. At degree level, hospitality and tourism management programmes run at UiTM (the largest public provider), Taylor's University (whose hospitality school ranks among the best in Asia), Sunway, Berjaya University College (backed by a group that owns hotels) and UMS in Sabah, where tourism is a flagship field. Funding comes from the usual national sources (JPA where fields align, PTPTN, state foundations) plus industry-linked support: Berjaya and other hospitality groups have sponsored students into their own programmes, and hotel chains run management trainee schemes that pay you while you learn the business after graduation. The TVET route is genuinely underrated: IKBN and community colleges run hospitality, culinary and tourism operations courses that are free or nearly free with allowances, and hotels hire straight from them. Starting pay in hotels is modest, around RM1,800 to RM2,500, but progression to supervisory and management roles is faster than most industries for people who stay, and the skills travel worldwide.

Carian pelancongan asyik pulang kosong di MyScholar, tandanya pelajar sangat mahukan halaman ini, jadi ini peta jujurnya. Industri pelancongan Malaysia kembali mengambil pekerja secara besar-besaran, dengan kempen Visit Malaysia, pembukaan hotel baru dan pengembangan syarikat penerbangan menarik graduan baru. Di peringkat ijazah, program pengurusan hospitaliti dan pelancongan ada di UiTM (penyedia awam terbesar), Taylor's University (sekolah hospitalitinya antara terbaik di Asia), Sunway, Berjaya University College (disokong kumpulan yang memiliki hotel) dan UMS di Sabah, di mana pelancongan bidang utama. Pembiayaan datang dari sumber nasional biasa (JPA untuk bidang berkaitan, PTPTN, yayasan negeri) serta sokongan berkait industri: Berjaya dan kumpulan hospitaliti lain pernah menaja pelajar ke program mereka sendiri, dan rangkaian hotel ada skim management trainee yang membayar anda sambil belajar selok-belok perniagaan selepas tamat. Laluan TVET sangat dipandang rendah: IKBN dan kolej komuniti menawarkan kursus hospitaliti, kulinari dan operasi pelancongan yang percuma atau hampir percuma dengan elaun, dan hotel mengambil terus dari situ. Gaji permulaan di hotel memang sederhana, sekitar RM1,800 hingga RM2,500, tetapi kenaikan ke jawatan penyeliaan dan pengurusan lebih pantas daripada kebanyakan industri untuk yang bertahan, dan kemahirannya laku di seluruh dunia.
