How to Become a DevOps Engineer in Malaysia

Cara Jadi Jurutera DevOps di Malaysia

DevOps sits between writing software and running it: engineers in this role build the pipelines that test and deploy code automatically, manage cloud infrastructure, and keep production systems alive at 3am so developers do not have to. In Malaysia the demand comes from everywhere at once: banks modernising core systems, GXBank and the digital banks built cloud-native from day one, fintechs, the MNC tech hubs in KL and Penang, and every company moving workloads onto AWS, Azure or Google Cloud. Almost nobody starts as a DevOps engineer straight out of university. The normal path is one to two years as a software developer or system administrator first, then a sideways move once you know how systems break. The skill stack that Malaysian job ads actually list: Linux fundamentals, Docker and Kubernetes, a CI/CD tool like GitLab CI or Jenkins, infrastructure-as-code with Terraform, one major cloud platform, and enough scripting (Python or Bash) to automate anything you do twice. Certifications help here more than in most tech roles because they map directly to tools: AWS Solutions Architect Associate and the Certified Kubernetes Administrator are the two with the most pull locally. Starting pay is around RM4,000 and moves fast: three years of solid Kubernetes and cloud experience routinely commands RM7,000 to RM9,000 in KL, and remote regional roles pay above that.

DevOps duduk di antara menulis perisian dan menjalankannya: jurutera dalam peranan ini bina pipeline yang menguji dan melancarkan kod secara automatik, mengurus infrastruktur cloud, dan memastikan sistem produksi hidup pukul 3 pagi supaya developer tidak perlu. Di Malaysia permintaan datang dari semua arah serentak: bank memodenkan sistem teras, GXBank dan bank digital dibina cloud-native dari hari pertama, fintech, hub teknologi MNC di KL dan Pulau Pinang, dan setiap syarikat yang memindahkan beban kerja ke AWS, Azure atau Google Cloud. Hampir tiada siapa mula sebagai jurutera DevOps terus dari universiti. Laluan biasa ialah satu hingga dua tahun sebagai software developer atau system administrator dulu, kemudian beralih setelah anda faham bagaimana sistem rosak. Susunan kemahiran yang iklan kerja Malaysia betul-betul senaraikan: asas Linux, Docker dan Kubernetes, alat CI/CD seperti GitLab CI atau Jenkins, infrastructure-as-code dengan Terraform, satu platform cloud utama, dan skrip (Python atau Bash) yang cukup untuk mengautomasikan apa sahaja yang anda buat dua kali. Pensijilan membantu di sini lebih daripada kebanyakan peranan teknologi kerana ia terus berkait dengan alat: AWS Solutions Architect Associate dan Certified Kubernetes Administrator dua yang paling laku tempatan. Gaji permulaan sekitar RM4,000 dan naik pantas: tiga tahun pengalaman Kubernetes dan cloud yang kukuh biasa dapat RM7,000 hingga RM9,000 di KL, dan kerja remote serantau bayar lebih tinggi.
