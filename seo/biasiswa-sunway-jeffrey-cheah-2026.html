Sunway and Jeffrey Cheah Foundation Scholarships

Biasiswa Sunway dan Jeffrey Cheah Foundation

Sunway's scholarship system is unusual among Malaysian private universities because of how it is owned: Sunway University and Sunway College operate under the Jeffrey Cheah Foundation, a structure where the institutions' surpluses are channelled back into education rather than out to shareholders, and the foundation has grown into one of the largest private education scholarship funders in the country. For students, that translates into a genuinely deep award structure. Entrance scholarships reward strong SPM, IGCSE, STPM, A-Level and UEC results with fee waivers that scale to full coverage for top performers, and the flagship awards for outstanding students can cover the entire programme. Beyond entry merit, the foundation runs community and hardship-based support, continuing scholarships that reward performance during study, and targeted awards across Sunway's schools, from business and hospitality (including the Le Cordon Bleu culinary partnership) to computing, engineering and the American Degree Transfer Program. Sunway's tie-ups add further routes: the Sunway TES accounting pathway carries its own high-achiever funding for ACCA-track students, and cross-registration links with partner institutions broaden what a scholar can study. Applications generally run alongside admissions, with results slips and co-curricular records as the working documents, and interviews for the higher tiers. If you are comparing private universities on net cost rather than sticker price, the foundation's depth means Sunway frequently lands cheaper than its brochure suggests for strong students, and the listings linked below show the current verified awards.

Sistem biasiswa Sunway luar biasa dalam kalangan universiti swasta Malaysia kerana cara pemilikannya: Sunway University dan Sunway College beroperasi di bawah Jeffrey Cheah Foundation, struktur di mana lebihan institusi disalurkan semula ke pendidikan dan bukan keluar kepada pemegang saham, dan yayasan ini telah berkembang menjadi antara penaja biasiswa pendidikan swasta terbesar di negara ini. Untuk pelajar, itu diterjemahkan kepada struktur anugerah yang benar-benar mendalam. Biasiswa kemasukan memberi ganjaran keputusan SPM, IGCSE, STPM, A-Level dan UEC yang kukuh dengan pengecualian yuran yang meningkat hingga tanggungan penuh untuk pencapai terbaik, dan anugerah utama untuk pelajar cemerlang boleh menanggung keseluruhan program. Selain merit kemasukan, yayasan ini menjalankan sokongan berasaskan komuniti dan kesusahan, biasiswa berterusan yang memberi ganjaran prestasi semasa belajar, dan anugerah disasar merentas sekolah-sekolah Sunway, dari perniagaan dan hospitaliti (termasuk kerjasama kulinari Le Cordon Bleu) hingga komputeran, kejuruteraan dan American Degree Transfer Program. Kerjasama Sunway menambah laluan lagi: laluan perakaunan Sunway TES membawa pembiayaan pencapai tinggi sendiri untuk pelajar trek ACCA, dan hubungan dengan institusi rakan meluaskan apa yang penerima boleh pelajari. Permohonan biasanya berjalan seiring kemasukan, dengan slip keputusan dan rekod kokurikulum sebagai dokumen kerja, dan temuduga untuk peringkat lebih tinggi. Kalau anda membandingkan universiti swasta ikut kos bersih dan bukan harga risalah, kedalaman yayasan ini bermakna Sunway kerap jatuh lebih murah daripada yang dijangka untuk pelajar cemerlang, dan senarai di bawah menunjukkan anugerah sah semasa.
