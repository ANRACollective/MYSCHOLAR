How to Become a Data Centre Engineer in Malaysia

Cara Jadi Jurutera Pusat Data di Malaysia

Malaysia is in the middle of a data centre construction boom that has few parallels anywhere. Johor, especially the Sedenak and Kulai corridor, has become one of the fastest growing data centre markets in the world, with hyperscalers and operators like Microsoft, Google, AWS, ByteDance, YTL and Bridge building or running campuses, alongside the established cluster in Cyberjaya. Every one of those facilities needs critical facility engineers and technicians around the clock, and operators openly say talent is their biggest constraint. The natural entry route is a degree in electrical or mechanical engineering, since the job is fundamentally about power systems, cooling, and uptime. Fresh graduates come in as facility or operations engineers around RM3,500 a month, and because sites run 24/7, shift allowances lift real income above that. Diploma and TVET holders in electrical, HVAC or chiller work enter as technicians and are in serious demand. Progression is fast for people who collect certifications like a chargeman ticket. This is also a career where being from Johor is suddenly an advantage: the jobs are landing there, not just in KL.

Malaysia sedang melalui ledakan pembinaan pusat data yang jarang ada tandingan. Johor, khususnya koridor Sedenak dan Kulai, kini antara pasaran pusat data paling pesat berkembang di dunia, dengan syarikat seperti Microsoft, Google, AWS, ByteDance, YTL dan Bridge membina atau mengendalikan kampus, di samping kluster sedia ada di Cyberjaya. Setiap fasiliti perlukan jurutera dan juruteknik fasiliti kritikal sepanjang masa, dan pengendali sendiri mengakui kekurangan tenaga kerja ialah kekangan terbesar mereka. Laluan masuk semula jadi ialah ijazah kejuruteraan elektrik atau mekanikal, kerana kerja ini asasnya tentang sistem kuasa, penyejukan, dan uptime. Graduan baru masuk sebagai jurutera fasiliti atau operasi sekitar RM3,500 sebulan, dan kerana tapak beroperasi 24/7, elaun syif menaikkan pendapatan sebenar. Pemegang diploma dan TVET dalam bidang elektrik, HVAC atau chiller masuk sebagai juruteknik dan sangat diperlukan. Kenaikan pangkat cepat untuk mereka yang kumpul sijil seperti chargeman. Ini juga kerjaya di mana berasal dari Johor tiba-tiba jadi kelebihan: kerja-kerja ini mendarat di sana, bukan hanya di KL.
