How to Become a Semiconductor Engineer in Malaysia

Cara Jadi Jurutera Semikonduktor di Malaysia

Semiconductors are Malaysia's biggest export industry, and the stretch from Bayan Lepas in Penang to Kulim Hi-Tech Park in Kedah is one of the densest chip manufacturing corridors in the world. Intel, Infineon, Micron, AMD, Bosch, ASE and dozens of local suppliers run wafer fabs, assembly and test plants there, and the National Semiconductor Strategy has pushed new investment into IC design as well, which is opening design centre roles in Penang, KL and Selangor that did not exist five years ago. The standard entry route is a degree in electrical and electronic engineering, mechatronics, or physics. USM in Penang feeds the industry directly, and UTM, UniMAP, MMU and UTAR graduates are heavily recruited too. Process, equipment, product, test and quality engineer roles start around RM3,800 a month for fresh graduates, with night shift and site allowances often pushing first year income higher. IC design pays the most, RM6,000 and above for those who master chip design tools during their degree. Diploma and TVET holders enter as technicians around RM2,500 and can work up. JPA, Yayasan Khazanah and several corporate scholarships name E&E as a priority field, and the big Penang employers run their own sponsorship and internship pipelines.

Semikonduktor ialah industri eksport terbesar Malaysia, dan kawasan dari Bayan Lepas di Pulau Pinang hingga Kulim Hi-Tech Park di Kedah antara koridor pembuatan cip paling padat di dunia. Intel, Infineon, Micron, AMD, Bosch, ASE dan puluhan pembekal tempatan beroperasi di situ, dan Strategi Semikonduktor Negara membawa pelaburan baru dalam reka bentuk IC, membuka peluang kerja design centre di Pulau Pinang, KL dan Selangor yang tidak wujud lima tahun lalu. Laluan masuk biasa ialah ijazah kejuruteraan elektrik dan elektronik, mekatronik, atau fizik. Graduan USM di Pulau Pinang masuk terus ke industri ini, dan graduan UTM, UniMAP, MMU dan UTAR juga banyak diambil. Jawatan jurutera proses, peralatan, produk, ujian dan kualiti bermula sekitar RM3,800 sebulan untuk graduan baru, dengan elaun syif malam dan tapak selalu menaikkan pendapatan tahun pertama. Reka bentuk IC bayar paling tinggi, RM6,000 ke atas untuk yang mahir alat reka bentuk cip semasa belajar. Pemegang diploma dan TVET masuk sebagai juruteknik sekitar RM2,500 dan boleh naik. JPA, Yayasan Khazanah dan beberapa biasiswa korporat menyenaraikan E&E sebagai bidang keutamaan, dan majikan besar Pulau Pinang ada saluran tajaan dan internship sendiri.
